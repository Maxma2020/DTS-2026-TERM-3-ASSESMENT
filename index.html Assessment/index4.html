Useful Websites for NZ Great Walks

Planning a Great Walk takes more than good boots. Here are a few websites that are genuinely worth bookmarking before you head out.

Department of Conservation (DOC)

The official government body managing all 11 Great Walks. This is where you book huts and campsites, check track conditions, and get up-to-date weather and safety alerts before setting off.

Visit doc.govt.nz →

MetService

New Zealand's national weather service. Mountain and backcountry weather can change fast, so checking regional forecasts here before and during your trip is a smart habit.

Visit metservice.com →

Mountain Safety Council

A non-profit focused on outdoor safety education. Their site has practical guides on gear, navigation, and river crossings that are especially handy for first-time trampers.

Visit mountainsafety.org.nz →

AllTrails

A community-driven trail app and website with user reviews, photos, and GPS tracks. Useful for cross-checking recent trail conditions reported by other hikers.

Visit alltrails.com →
