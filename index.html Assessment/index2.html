The Steps of NZ Walkways

The New Zealand Land Safety Code was developed by the New Zealand Mountain Safety Council, Department of Conservation, Land Search and Rescue, Rescue Coordination Centre New Zealand, New Zealand Police, MetService, Auckland Council, and New Zealand Search and Rescue. It includes the following five essential steps for staying safe outdoors.

Step 1

Its important to choose a trip that is suitable for everyone in your group. Know how long it should take and the way to go. For example, make sure you find out if there are any difficult sections, such as a river crossing
Step 2

The weather is one of the most important things to consider. It can change fast, so ensure that you check the New Zealand weather forecast again before you leave. Have a back-up plan and be prepared to change your route due to weather if necessary.
Step 3

Use the PlanMyWalk.nz app to see the essential gear list for your trip. Dont forget to prepare for both hot and cold weather, and an unexpected night out in the open. Some outdoor stores will hire out clothing and equipment if you dont have your own.
Step 4

Share your information with someone you trust before you go. You can also record your information on the PlanMyWalk.nz app. Many tracks in New Zealand dont have mobile phone reception, so consider taking a distress beacon (PLB/Personal Locator Beacon). And dont forget to sign the DOC intentions book in each hut as you pass.
